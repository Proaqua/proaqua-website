Do You Have Any of These
AC Problems at Home?

Before you book anyone, check the room you use most. These are the signs Franco asks families to look for first.

Bad odours

Unpleasant smells coming from your vents when the AC starts.

Water leakage

Dripping or pooling water around your AC unit or vent area.

Poor cooling

The room never reaches the right temperature, even with AC running.

High humidity

Sticky, damp air even while the AC is on and the room is closed.

Dust buildup

Excessive dust settling around vents, grills or nearby furniture.

Allergy symptoms

Sneezing, coughing or irritation increases when you stay indoors.

Mould smell or signs

Musty mould smell or visible dark marks near the AC system.

Unusual noises

Strange sounds coming from the AC unit, ducts or vents.

These may be signs that your AC system has not received proper maintenance.

How often should I clean my AC ducts in Dubai? +
Due to Dubai's dusty climate and year-round AC usage, we recommend every 12 months. Homes with pets, young children or allergy sufferers benefit from every 6–9 months.
What is included in the free inspection? +
Our technician visits your home, inspects all AC units, vents and ducts, and provides a full photo report. No charge, no obligation — we show you exactly what's inside.
How fast can I get an appointment? +
We are active 7 days a week and usually reply on WhatsApp within minutes. Depending on your area and current schedule, inspections can often be arranged within 24–48 hours — message us and we will offer the fastest available slot.
Is the cleaning safe for children and pets? +
Yes. We use professional equipment and residential-use cleaning products, and we confirm suitability for homes with children and pets before we start.
How long does AC duct cleaning take? +
A 1–2 bedroom apartment takes 2–3 hours. Villas may take 4–6 hours. We always provide a time estimate upfront.
Do you offer a satisfaction guarantee? +
Yes. We document every job with before and after photos. If you're not satisfied, we return and re-clean at no additional charge.
