FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my AC ducts in Dubai? +
Due to Dubai's dusty climate and year-round AC usage, we recommend every 12 months. Homes with pets, young children or allergy sufferers benefit from every 6–9 months.
What is included in the free inspection? +
Our technician visits your home, inspects all AC units, vents and ducts, and provides a full photo report. No charge, no obligation — we show you exactly what's inside.
Is the cleaning safe for children and pets? +
Yes, completely. We use certified, non-toxic products and medical-grade equipment approved for residential use — safe for children, babies and pets.
How long does AC duct cleaning take? +
A 1–2 bedroom apartment takes 2–3 hours. Villas may take 4–6 hours. We always provide a time estimate upfront.
Do you offer a satisfaction guarantee? +
Yes. We document every job with before and after photos. If you're not satisfied, we return and re-clean at no additional charge.
How do I book and what payment do you accept? +
WhatsApp us at +971 56 794 3412 — we confirm within minutes. We accept card payment, cash and bank transfer. Payment details provided after inspection.
