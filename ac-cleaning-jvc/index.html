Why JVC AC systems need a regular clean

JVC (Jumeirah Village Circle) is a large, still-growing community laid out in numbered districts, starting at District 10. It mixes mid-rise apartment buildings, townhouses and a few villa pockets, so AC systems range from one split unit to full villa setups.

New buildings keep going up around the circle. That means construction dust reaches filters, coils and grilles faster than in older, settled areas, and many residents see dark marks at the vents within the first year or two.

Because JVC homes vary so much, we ask three things before quoting: building or street, AC type and unit count. With that we can tell you what the visit looks like and give a fixed price.

AC Cleaning FAQ in JVC

Do you offer air duct cleaning in JVC, Dubai? +
Yes. AC duct and grille cleaning is our core service in JVC, Dubai. A basic clean is AED 400 per unit and a full deep clean AED 800 per unit, plus VAT. After the free check you get a fixed quote.
Do you clean AC in JVC townhouses and apartments? +
Yes, both. Townhouses usually need more time because of the number of units.
Which JVC districts do you cover? +
All numbered districts, including District 19 (Circle Villas). Tell us the building or street.
Does construction dust affect the AC? +
Dust can reach filters, coils and grilles. We inspect and tell you what we see.
Do I need a permit? +
Some buildings and communities do. Check with your management and tell us when you book.
What does it cost? +
It depends on unit count, type and condition. You get a fixed quote first.

A Pro Aqua technician checks the visible AC units, vents, grilles and service scope, then explains what needs attention. The inspection is free and has no obligation. Before any work starts, you receive a clear fixed quote and we document the result with photos.
Which Dubai areas does Pro Aqua cover? +
Pro Aqua Dubai serves all major Dubai communities, including Dubai Marina, JBR, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Jumeirah, Arabian Ranches, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Hills, JVC, Al Barsha, Mirdif and surrounding areas across the emirate.
How do you know if there is mould in my AC ducts? +
Common signs of mould-related buildup include a musty smell when the AC starts, dark marks around vents that return after cleaning, or a heavier, damp feeling in the room. Franco's team inspects visible AC points first and explains what they find before recommending any treatment.
Can a dirty AC system increase my DEWA electricity bill? +
A clogged or contaminated AC system often has to work harder to reach the same temperature, which can increase electricity consumption over time. While Pro Aqua does not give specific savings guarantees, many customers report the AC running more efficiently after a documented duct cleaning.
What are the signs that my AC ducts need cleaning? +
Common signs include musty or stale air from vents, visible dust around AC grilles, weak airflow, poor cooling, or dust returning quickly after cleaning. If you notice black marks or a mould-like smell around vents, book a professional inspection before assuming it is only surface dust.
What is the difference between AC filter cleaning and AC duct cleaning? +
Filter cleaning is basic maintenance and only addresses the removable filter. AC duct cleaning checks and cleans deeper parts of the air path, including ducts, grilles and related buildup that filters cannot remove. In Dubai, dust and construction particles can build up quickly, so filter cleaning alone is not always enough.
